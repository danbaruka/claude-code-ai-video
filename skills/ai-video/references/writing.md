# Writing the script

A short video is won or lost in its first 2 seconds, then again at every sentence: the thumb can swipe at any moment.
This guide comes from:
- an analysis of French-speaking tech and AI accounts that took off in October 2026;
- our own numbers.

It applies to any language.

## Structure (45 to 70 s, 110 to 170 words, 9 to 14 sentences)

| # | Role | Length | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The hook**: the topic, stated plainly, with tension | 2-3 s | "Your crypto is not in your wallet." |
| 2 | **The proof or the stakes**, so people believe you and stay | 3-5 s | "Over 95,000 stars on GitHub in one year." |
| 3-9 | **The content**: 3 to 5 concrete points, one per sentence or two | 30-45 s | "The first one is… It does…" |
| 10 | **The nuance or the catch**, which keeps it honest | 4-6 s | "Not yet. The new version is still being built." |
| 11 | **The line to remember** | 3 s | "A wallet is a keyring, not a safe." |
| 12 | **What's next**: a reason to follow, tied to the topic. Optional: with a signed `fin` card, the line to remember is enough. | 3 s | "Follow for more tools like this." |

## Hooks that work

These formulas come from videos with 10,000 to 50,000 views.

- **The contrarian**: "People say X. It's wrong."
  > People say Claude is a bad designer. It's wrong. It's just missing 4 tools.
- **The solved problem**: "The problem with X is that… Well, that was before."
- **The wrong culprit**: "To get [result] without [pain], you don't need A or B. You need N things first."
- **The only one**: "If you could install only one tool for X, it would be this one."
- **The face-off**: "Did X just kill Y?" The answer comes at the end, with nuance: "Not yet. But Y should be worried."
- **The obstacle removed**: "Your client doesn't have WhatsApp? You can still call them on WhatsApp."
- **The ladder**: "If you do A, you're in the top 80%. If you also do B, the top 20%. If C, the top 1%." It works for any list of good practices.
- **The number**: "2.2 billion dollars stolen in one year. Here's how they do it."
- **The result**: "I've been doing X for two weeks. Here are the results." Then give the real numbers.

Avoid:
- "Imagine…";
- "In this video, I'm going to…";
- a vague question ("Do you know X?");
- a hook that promises more than the video delivers.

## Topics that take off in tech

Ranked by views:
1. **Specific tools**, named, for a tool the audience already uses: 4 plugins for Claude Code, 4 tools for better design.
2. **A new feature in a mainstream app**, and how to use it: WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, banking apps.
3. **"Did X kill Y?"**: a free or open-source tool against a well-known paid one.
4. **Scams and thefts**, with a number and a concrete protection.
5. **A method with numbers**: "rank #1 on Google", "the 12-word rule".

What gets few views:
- technical security alerts (PHP, GitLab flaws), unless the audience is developers;
- "Top 5 apps nobody talks about" series;
- videos about yourself or your product, before you have an audience.

## Writing rules

- **Clarity first.**
  - The topic must be understood from sentence 1.
  - A technical word is explained right away: "the blockchain, a big public ledger".
- **One idea per sentence**, 8 to 18 words. The voice breathes between sentences, and each sentence can carry its own scene.
- **Be concrete.**
  - Use tool names, numbers and gestures: "open the Calls tab, tap +".
  - Never "lots of features".
- **Give proof**: GitHub stars, a release date, "official", the team's name.
  - Check every number: a mistake shows up in the comments.
- **One pronoun throughout.** In French, "vous" or "tu", never both.
- **No made-up characters.** Talk to the viewer with concrete examples. An invented first name sounds fake.
- **Write numbers as they are spoken** when the voice stumbles ("twelve words"), and as digits in scenes (`"valeur": "12"`).
- **Acronyms and foreign names** go through `prononcer` (`"AI": "A I"`, `"Ledger": "Ledjer"`). Never change the text shown on screen for them.
- **The ending.**
  - It asks no vague question: it gives the line to remember, then what's next.
  - To get comments, use a keyword: "Comment WALLET and I'll send you the list." Only do it if you really reply.

## Title, caption, pinned comment (`publication`)

| Key | What to write |
|---|---|
| `titre` (YouTube) | under 60 characters, the promise, no "🔥" |
| `legende` | 1 to 3 sentences that restate the hook differently, then 3 to 5 precise hashtags (`crypto`, `wallet`), not `fyp` |
| `commentaire` | pinned within the first minute: a real follow-up question, or the promised resource |
